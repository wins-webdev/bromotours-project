ABOUT US

MORE THAN A DESTINATION. AN EXPERIENCE TO REMEMBER.

“We believe every journey should be more than just reaching a destination. It should be about discovering new perspectives, connecting with local culture, and creating memories that last a lifetime.”

Too many travelers visit Mount Bromo and Ijen Crater on rushed, crowded conveyor-belt buses—arriving late at packed railings and leaving before the mist even lifts from the caldera. We founded BROMO TOURS to offer the exact opposite: unhurried, deeply atmospheric expeditions across Mount Bromo’s Sea of Sand and Ijen’s glowing blue-fire crater lake, crafted around natural light, quieter ridge viewpoints, and genuine connection with East Java’s highland communities.

The dry season from April to October offers the crispest skies for golden sunrises and Milky Way astrophotography. However, the green shoulder months (November to March) transform the Bromo Savanna and Teletubbies Hill into vibrant emerald valleys with dramatic moody clouds and fewer crowds.

Pre-dawn temperatures at 2,700 meters frequently drop between 3°C and 8°C (37°F–46°F) with brisk mountain winds. Bring a warm insulated jacket or fleece layers, long trousers, sturdy closed-toe walking shoes with good grip, a beanie or gloves, sunglasses, a dust mask or buff for the volcanic Sea of Sand, and your camera with extra batteries.
